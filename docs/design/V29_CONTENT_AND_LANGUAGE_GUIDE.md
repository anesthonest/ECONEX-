## 1. Core Positioning & Universal Voice

ECONEX serves ordinary individuals, smallholder farmers, retail merchants, artisans, logistics fleets, institutions, and enterprises equally across **28+ economic sectors**.

### Voice Principles
1. **Universal Over Sector-Specific:** Never frame the homepage, marketplace, or core workflows as exclusively agricultural. Lead with multi-sector opportunity language (`"One platform. Every sector. More opportunities."`, `"Browse verified goods, services, tools, and supplies"`).
2. **Action Over Jargon:** Replace internal state-machine labels and architecture jargon on user-facing screens with plain human actions.
3. **Calm, Direct & Respectful:** Short, helpful microcopy suited for first-time smartphone users as well as institutional procurement managers.
4. **Strict Truthfulness & Sandbox Clarity:** Financial limits (`Sandbox simulation active`), location scope (`selected district` vs verified GPS), and unprovisioned external rails are stated clearly in plain words where financial or identity actions occur — never buried and never overstated as live settlement.

## 3. Audience Separation (Ordinary Users vs Administrators/Developers)

- **Ordinary Users (`SIMPLE` & `STANDARD` experience levels):** See `Business`, `Trade`, `Finance`, `Network`, and `Support` menus in plain everyday wording, with zero raw `/api` paths, schema numbers, or test assertion counters.
- **Enterprise & Institutional Operators (`PROFESSIONAL` level):** Access cross-border corridors, warehouse ATP, procurement 3-way matching, and BOM manufacturing tools inside clearly labeled `Business` and `Trade` groups.
- **Platform Administrators & Engineers (`ADMINISTRATOR` level + `PLATFORM_ADMIN` role):** Access `Administration` and `Technical & Developer Library` containing Schema 26 diagnostics, interactive control audits, release notes, and export manifests.
